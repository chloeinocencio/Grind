# Grind — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Grind does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Your lists stay on your device.** Daily goals, ongoing goals, and pomodoro settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to a server.
- **No accounts.** Grind has no sign-up or login.
- **No tracking or analytics.** Grind doesn't use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
- **Notifications are local.** If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Deleting the app removes all of its data.
